# Outreach voice samples

These are the canonical voice exemplars Claude uses when generating personalized
outreach. They reflect the structure + tone that survived review feedback from
Samantha, Marisol, Mark, and Meredith.

Key principles these samples demonstrate:
1. Subject line tells the reader the purpose of the email before they open it.
2. Opening paragraph introduces sender AND states the purpose (referral partnership).
3. Complete sentences throughout — no fragments. Warm but professional.
4. One specific reference to the recipient. Tailored two-houses pitch.
5. Clear ask + reciprocity. Soft CTA with the reply path.

---

## Exemplar 1 — to a 30A private chef

Subject: Sun & Moon at 30A — possible chef partnership

Hi there,

My name is Cristian Orihuela. I own Sun & Moon at 30A — two adjacent vacation rental houses on Crystal Court in Seagrove Beach — and I'm reaching out because I'd love to explore a referral partnership with Marrow Private Chefs.

Looking at your reviews, it's clear you're one of the most-loved private chefs on 30A. The setup we offer might be relevant to your work, because it's unusual: there are two full kitchens between our adjacent houses, two ovens, and a shared backyard that connects both properties. For groups of 12 to 16 staying together, that means a chef has real prep space — you can stage in one kitchen, plate from the other, and serve out in the yard.

If you would be open to it, I'd love to send you the listings and put you on the welcome vendor sheet we share with every guest. In return, any group that books with us and asks about a chef would hear about you first. The easiest next step is a quick reply, or you can reach me at experience@sunandmoon30a.com.

Thanks for considering it.

Best,
Cristian

## Exemplar 2 — to a 30A wedding planner

Subject: Sun & Moon at 30A — vacation rental partnership idea

Hi there,

I'm Cristian Orihuela, owner of Sun & Moon at 30A — two adjacent vacation rental houses on Crystal Court in Seagrove Beach. I'm writing because I'd love to explore a referral partnership with your wedding planning business.

Our setup is something I think your couples would find appealing. The two houses sit next door to each other and sleep sixteen combined, which means the bride's family can take one house and the groom's family can take the other, and everyone gets both privacy and togetherness under one booking and one address. It is a simpler logistical setup than coordinating multiple Airbnbs for the rehearsal-dinner-through-Sunday-brunch flow that destination weddings usually require. The yards connect between the houses, so there is a shared outdoor space for getting-ready mornings and rehearsal-dinner gatherings.

If our houses would fit any of your upcoming bookings, I'd love to be on your list of housing options. In return, any couple who reaches out to us without a planner would hear about your work first. You can reply directly or reach me at experience@sunandmoon30a.com.

Thanks for the time.

Best,
Cristian
