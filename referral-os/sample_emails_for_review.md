# Sample outreach emails — 10 for review

Ten personalized first-touch emails, hand-crafted using the data we have on each partner. Mix of segments (chefs, local wedding planners, drive-market planners, photographer, bachelorette concierge) so you see the range. CTA on every email is `experience@sunandmoon30a.com`.

**Voice goals:** warm, casual, southern-friendly, brief. Sounds like a real person running a small business writing to another real person. Not corporate. Not pushy.

**The two-adjacent-houses angle is tailored per partner type** — for chefs it's about the two kitchens + shared yard; for wedding planners it's about splitting bride's/groom's families across the two houses; for photographers it's about location variety; for bachelorette it's about group size and privacy. Same fact, different framing.

## Notes for Samantha

**Patterns to react to:**

1. **The two-adjacent-houses angle is framed differently for each partner type** — for chefs it's the two-kitchen story; for wedding planners it's the bride's/groom's family split; for photographers it's location variety; for bachelorette it's the awkward group size. Same physical fact, segment-specific framing.

2. **Every email has the same five-part structure:**
   - 1-line intro (who I am)
   - Why I'm reaching out (1 sentence referencing them specifically)
   - The two-houses pitch tuned to their world (the "meat")
   - The ask + reciprocity (be on your list / I'll send referrals back)
   - CTA to `experience@sunandmoon30a.com`

3. **Length:** 130-170 words. Reads in under 30 seconds.

4. **Voice:** casual, neighborly, no salesy language, no "I'd love to schedule a 30-min discovery call." Just "reply or email us."

5. **What's missing:** Honestly evaluate whether the pitch is compelling enough. Are we explaining the "two houses" angle clearly? Should we mention specific differentiators (private pools? walking distance to beach? sleeping arrangements)? Should the CTA be softer or harder?

**Things we should probably standardize after Samantha reviews:**

- Should we always sign off "— Cristian" or "— Cristian Orihuela"?
- Should we include a P.S. with one specific differentiator (e.g., "P.S. — both houses have heated pools")?
- Should we add a 1-line signature with the website URL?
- Is "experience@sunandmoon30a.com" the right address, or should outreach replies go to a personal address?
- Should we include attachment-style links to a one-pager PDF instead of asking them to email for details?

**Data quality observation:**

While building these I noticed our segment labels are imperfect — some partners are tagged in multiple segments (Kiss the Bride is in both `wedding-planner-local` AND `bachelorette-planner`, which is actually correct since wedding planners often do bach weekends too). Worth a future pass to confirm partners are in the right primary segment for outreach.
