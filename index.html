Welcome

Where every day begins with the sun and ends with the moon.

Tucked into a quiet pocket of Seagrove Beach on Scenic 30A, Sun & Moon at 30A is two beautifully appointed coastal cottages — Blue Moon and Golden Sun — sitting just steps apart on Crystal Court, three minutes' walk to the emerald waters of the Gulf.


Book one cottage for an intimate getaway, or take both for a full-property family compound. Six bedrooms, four baths, room for sixteen — and a setup that gives every group the rare gift of being together and apart whenever they choose.

The 30A Area

A short walk from everything that makes 30A famous.

Seagrove sits in the quietest pocket of Scenic 30A — between the picture-book pedestrian town of Seaside and the laid-back lakes-and-dunes village of WaterColor. Both are a short drive (or a quick golf-cart ride from the rental shop around the corner). Drive to Alys Beach in fifteen.

The whole stretch is built for slowing down: 26 miles of beach-cruiser path, hidden coastal dune lakes, neighborhood farmers markets, an outdoor amphitheater with weekly concerts, and a calendar that quietly delivers world-class festivals all year long.
